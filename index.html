PRAYAAN — AI-Powered Urban Intelligence Platform

Category: Software | Team Leader: Rishab C V

Concept & Core Value

Turns Chennai's public bus fleet into a moving sensor network[cite: 2, 3]. Edge modules on-board each bus run lightweight ML inference and stream classified events to a central backend for live urban governance[cite: 2, 3].

5 Layers of Route Sensing[cite: 2, 3]

  • GPS & Congestion: Real-time positions and delay heatmaps[cite: 2, 3].
  • Air Quality (AQI): Continuous mapping of PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, CO, O₃[cite: 2, 3].
  • Potholes: Vibration-ML classifier with GPS tagging[cite: 2, 3].
  • Civic Issues: Vision-based waterlogging and street light fault flags[cite: 2, 3].
  • Fleet Health: Battery and module uptime heartbeats[cite: 2, 3].

Architecture & Data Scale[cite: 2, 3]

FastAPI Backend (Port 8000) handling REST + WebSockets connected to SQLite (`prayaan.db`) and React Leaflet.js dashboard[cite: 2, 3]. Simulated using 50 buses across 10 MTC Chennai routes generating ~55,000 events/day[cite: 2, 3].

Smart Cities Research Initiative

Pollution-Adaptive Intelligent Street Lighting System

Research Field: IoT, Environmental Sensing, Energy Conservation

System Objective & Innovation

Conventional smart streetlights only adjust brightness based on time or ambient light. This system incorporates air quality index (AQI) and particulate density sensors to dynamically penetrate thick smog and fog while dialing down brightness during clear conditions to maximize municipal energy savings.

Key Research Sub-Topics

  • Atmospheric Optical Density Sensing: Micro-controller interface with PM2.5/PM10 sensors to calculate real-time roadway visibility index.
  • Dynamic PWM Spectral Tuning: Automated adjustment of pulse-width modulation (PWM) drivers to shift light temperature and intensity for optimal human vision through particulate haze.
  • Grid-Wide Energy Optimization: Distributed controller algorithm minimizing grid power draw across municipal lighting grids.
