Research Field: IoT, Environmental Sensing, Energy Conservation[cite: 8]

System Objective & Innovation

Conventional smart streetlights only adjust brightness based on time or ambient light[cite: 8]. This system incorporates air quality index (AQI) and particulate density sensors to dynamically penetrate thick smog and fog while dialing down brightness during clear conditions to maximize municipal energy savings[cite: 8].

Key Research Sub-Topics

  • Atmospheric Optical Density Sensing: Micro-controller interface with PM2.5/PM10 sensors to calculate real-time roadway visibility index[cite: 8].
  • Dynamic PWM Spectral Tuning: Automated adjustment of pulse-width modulation (PWM) drivers to shift light temperature and intensity for optimal human vision through particulate haze[cite: 8].
  • Grid-Wide Energy Optimization: Distributed controller algorithm minimizing grid power draw across municipal lighting grids[cite: 8].
